## 001 - Two Men Died Over a Roll of Duct Tape

- **Incident:** Valero Delaware City refinery, November 5, 2005. Two contract workers asphyxiated by nitrogen.
- **Source:** CSB Case Study 2006-02-I-DE (22 pages).
- **Why this one:** picked from all 134 completed CSB investigations. One invisible cause, a
  would-be-rescuer second act, an absurdly small trigger (a roll of tape), and a lesson that
  applies to anyone who works near a tank, pit or vessel.
- **Format:** Short, 58.5 s, 177 words at 186 wpm.
- **Hook:** "Two men died over this roll of duct tape." Object-first, first frame is the object.
- **Tested variable:** none; this is the baseline everything else is measured against.
- **Results:** pending.

### What to watch when the numbers come in

- Retention at 3 s (does the hook hold?), at ~20 s (the permit beat is the slowest stretch), and at
  ~50 s (do people stay for the tribute?).
- Candidates for the first test, one at a time: a question hook vs this statement hook; 45 s vs 58 s;
  title with the number first ("2 Men, 1 Roll of Duct Tape").
